I'm especially interested in roles at the intersection of ML and clinical work. My research uses neural decoding and source localization — deep learning that pinpoints where seizures begin from scalp EEG — with the goal of improving the precision of epilepsy surgery.

One thing my PhD has taught me is that ML scientists often focus on metrics and models and can lose sight of real clinical needs. A role that bridges the two — rigorous modeling in genuine service of patients — is exactly what I care about.
